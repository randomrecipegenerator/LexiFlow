AI-Powered Document Intelligence for Mass Tort Litigation

Discovery-Vault\u2122 is an AI document intelligence platform purpose-built for mass tort and multi-district litigation firms. It transforms the manual process of reviewing thousands of discovery documents \u2014 medical records, military records, internal communications, scientific studies \u2014 into an automated, searchable evidence repository with contradiction detection and key-fact extraction.

How Discovery-Vault Processes Discovery Documents

The system uses a three-stage pipeline: Ingest \u2014 upload documents in bulk and the system automatically classifies each document by type and category. Index \u2014 every document is indexed page-by-page with full-text search, metadata extraction, and integrity verification. Analyze \u2014 the AI performs cross-document comparison to identify contradictions, extract critical facts, and surface high-value evidence with source citations.

Frequently Asked Questions About Discovery-Vault

Last updated: August 14, 2026

What is Discovery-Vault and how does it work for mass tort litigation?

Discovery-Vault is an AI-powered document intelligence platform built for mass tort and MDL litigation. It ingests thousands of discovery documents, indexes them by category, runs contradiction detection across the evidentiary record, and extracts key facts tied to specific page sources. Law firms upload documents once and the system processes them for relevance, chain-of-custody integrity, and evidentiary value.

Can Discovery-Vault handle multi-district litigation document volumes?

Yes. Discovery-Vault is designed specifically for MDL-scale document volumes. The system processes at scale and tracks evidence composition across categories such as military records, medical records, and scientific studies. Active litigation portfolios are updated in real time with document completion status, facts found, and processing health metrics.

How does Discovery-Vault ensure evidence integrity?

Every document uploaded to Discovery-Vault undergoes automated integrity verification. The system tracks chain of custody, assigns integrity scores per document category, and surfaces any discrepancies between document content and metadata. All extracted facts include source citations for quick verification during trial preparation.

How does Discovery-Vault compare to traditional e-discovery tools?

Traditional e-discovery tools focus on search and review workflow. Discovery-Vault adds AI-powered contradiction detection, cross-document fact extraction, and automated evidence composition analysis. Instead of manually comparing documents, the system surfaces critical connections and inconsistencies across the entire evidentiary record in seconds. For transcript analysis, see Veritas Deposition\u2122.
