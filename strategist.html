AI Case Strategy and Litigation Intelligence Suite

LexiFlow Strategist\u2122 is a suite of four original AI tools purpose-built for plaintiff law firms. It combines reasoning-based case analysis with automated strategy generation to help attorneys evaluate liability, prepare cross-examinations, and track procedural deadlines \u2014 all from a single workspace.

The Four AI Tools in LexiFlow Strategist

  • Life Care Plan AI
    Generates comprehensive life care plans for catastrophic injury cases, including future medical cost projections, therapy schedules, and equipment needs.
  • Opposing Counsel AI
    Profiles opposing counsel by name, firm, and practice area. Surfaces historical settlement behavior, defense strategies, case timelines, and verdict history.
  • SOL Guardian
    Statute of Limitations tracker with automated deadline calculations, jurisdiction-specific rules, and calendar integration to prevent missed filing dates.
  • Trial Readiness Score
    Analyzes case preparation across evidence, witness, and procedural readiness dimensions, producing a 0-100 score with actionable recommendations.

How the Strategic Analysis Engine Works

The engine ingests case facts, deposition testimony, medical records, and discovery responses to identify evidentiary weaknesses, impeachment opportunities, and jurisdictional factors. Each analysis generates a liability score (0-100), AI-generated cross-examination outlines, and a subpoena roadmap for additional evidence gathering. The system surfaces specific contradictions with source citations and suggests targeted questions for deposition or trial.

Frequently Asked Questions About LexiFlow Strategist

Last updated: August 14, 2026

What is LexiFlow Strategist and what tools does it include?

LexiFlow Strategist is a suite of four original AI tools for law firms: Life Care Plan AI for catastrophic injury life care plans, Opposing Counsel AI for profiling opposing counsel, SOL Guardian for automated statute of limitations tracking, and Trial Readiness Score for analyzing case preparation with a 0-100 readiness score.

How does the Liability Score work in LexiFlow Strategist?

The Liability Score (0-100) is computed from 12 sub-factors across three categories: Evidence Strength (50% of total) including direct evidence of fault, independent witness support, and expert report strength; Legal Merits (30%) including standard of care clarity, proximate causation, and statute of limitations; Risk Assessment (20%) including plaintiff credibility risks and comparative fault exposure.

Can LexiFlow Strategist generate cross-examination questions?

Yes. The Strategist generates AI-powered cross-examination outlines based on case-specific evidentiary weaknesses and contradictions. Questions follow standard impeachment protocols and are organized by theme for deposition or trial use. The system identifies key contradictions and generates targeted questions to exploit each inconsistency.

How does Opposing Counsel AI work?

Opposing Counsel AI profiles opposing counsel based on name, firm, and practice area to help attorneys develop targeted litigation strategies.
